Independent Project · Summer 2026

Where Do My
Taxes Go?

Ever wondered exactly how much you spend on K-12 education each year? This tool helps North Carolina residents see how their state, county, and local taxes are collected and where that money is actually spent. Users can estimate their annual tax burden by county, income bracket, and housing status across property, sales, state income, federal income, and payroll taxes, then trace that estimate directly into spending categories like education, public safety, and infrastructure.

Independent Project · Spring 2026

NC County
Financial Explorer

I built this benchmarking tool after discussions with budget analysts from across North Carolina. Before this tool, county financial data had to be downloaded in .xlsx form one-by-one from the treasurer's website to be viewable. This made benchmarking a tedious and tiring experience. Using Claude Code and Codex to help, I built an interactive data visualization of North Carolina county finances covering all 100 counties with revenue and expenditure breakdowns, per capita comparisons, choropleth mapping, and multi-year trend analysis.

Paragon Policy Fellowship · Fall 2025

AI EdTech Evaluation
for NYC Public Schools

Like all school districts, NYC Public Schools is curious about the impact AI may have on K-12 education. As the nation's largest school district (1M+ students, $38B budget), they are uniquely flooded with pitches and demo requests from vendors selling unvetted tools. Through the Paragon Policy Fellowship, I led a team of nine in developing an AI procurement and evaluation framework for NYC Public Schools to help them stay current while avoiding AI snake oil. Our team delivered market segmentation analysis, stakeholder mapping, and an original AI Procurement Scoring Matrix to support district leadership in making evidence-based tech investment decisions at scale.
